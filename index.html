🎲 The big question for the room

iOS is out of learning after 90 plus days. Is that actually good for us?

iOS has been dark since May, so the learnings have faded. Normally that hurts. But those learnings were built while COPPA was broken and iOS was wrongly flagged as restricted, so the old signal is polluted, not valuable.

A clean start on fixed signal might be a better place to begin than dragging that corrupted history forward. The trade is we re enter the learning phase and need budget and strong creative to exit it fast, especially since they want to launch D28 right away.

The call: reset to scratch, or keep. I lean reset, but I want the PfM view.
